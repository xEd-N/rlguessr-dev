Specials

Experimental mini-games — still deciding which of these stay.

Earnings Rank No clues but their face and tag. Rank the pack from highest to lowest career earnings.
Career Era All the usual clues, but the roster is cut down to players active before or after RLCS Season X.
Regional Scene Same clues as Classic, but the roster is cut down to players currently competing in one region.
10 players Quick Ten Same clues as Classic, but just a short run of 10 random players — then a recap and a fresh set.
LAN Roster Same clues as Classic, but the roster is cut down to everyone — 3v3, 1v1, 2v2 — who competed at one LAN.
